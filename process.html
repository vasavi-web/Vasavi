My Work Process

A clear, structured workflow designed to keep your project smooth, predictable, and professional from start to finish.

1. Discovery

Understanding your goals, audience, and requirements so the project starts with clarity.

2. Planning

Creating a clear structure, sitemap, content plan, and technical outline before design begins.

3. Design

Building a clean, modern interface with consistent spacing, typography, and brand feel.

4. Development

Turning the design into a fully functional, responsive website using clean and fast code.

5. Revisions

Refining the design and functionality based on your feedback until everything feels right.

6. Delivery

Final website launch with performance optimization, security setup, and training if needed.

7. Support

Ongoing help with updates, improvements, and any issues that come up after launch.
