Case Study 3

E-commerce (WhatsApp Order)

Overview

A friction-free e-commerce solution for small businesses that prefer direct WhatsApp orders.

Business Goals

  • Fastest possible order flow (no checkout)
  • Zero learning curve for customers
  • Auto-filled WhatsApp messages with product details
  • Ideal for COD + local businesses

Why Small Sellers Choose This

No cart abandonment. No complicated forms. Customers simply choose a product → tap WhatsApp → and place an order.
